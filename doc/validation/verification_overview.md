> **Intended Audience:** HW Integrators, HW Devs

The CoralNPU IP is rigorously verified through a multi-tiered verification strategy designed to ensure architectural compliance, structural integrity, and high-performance operation.

## Simulation

The primary verification environment leverages **Cocotb** paired with cycle-accurate simulators (like Verilator or VCS).

- **AXI Agents:** Python-based AXI master and slave agents drive high-stress traffic to the NPU memory interfaces, mimicking a busy SoC crossbar.
- **Instruction Testing:** Small assembly sequences (`.S` files) are compiled and loaded into the simulator to test specific vector and scalar instructions.

## RISC-V architectural testing

The core's scalar and vector pipelines are validated against standard RISC-V Architectural Tests (RV-ARCH-TEST). Any deviation from the official RV32I or RVV specifications triggers a failure.

## Mutation testing

To prevent "greenwashing" (tests that pass without making meaningful assertions), the verification pipeline utilizes RTL mutation testing. Small, adversarial changes are injected into the RTL logic; if the testbench continues to pass despite the mutation, the test is flagged as insufficient and requires remediation.
